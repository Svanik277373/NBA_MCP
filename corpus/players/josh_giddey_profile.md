# Josh Giddey — 2024-25 Scouting Profile

Giddey's game is built around processing speed rather than explosiveness.
He is one of the best open-court decision-makers at his position, routinely
grabbing a defensive rebound and pushing the ball himself rather than
outletting to a guard — a rare skill for a player his size. His court
vision on the move is advanced: he consistently finds cutters and shooters
in early offense before the defense sets.

The jump shot remains the swing skill. Mechanics have improved year over
year — a higher release point and more consistent base — and the catch-and-shoot
numbers have followed, but the pull-up three off movement is still below
league average, which limits how much defenses respect him beyond the arc
in half-court sets. Teams have started going under screens against him,
daring him to shoot.

Defensively, Giddey's lack of elite lateral quickness shows up against
quicker, shiftier guards, but his length and instincts make him a good team
defender and an above-average defensive rebounder for a perimeter player.
He projects best next to a rim-protecting big who can cover for his
occasional gambling for steals.

A change of scenery has been good for his role clarity — he is being used
more deliberately as a secondary ball-handler and less as a primary
initiator, which plays to his strengths as a connective passer rather than
a shot-creator.
