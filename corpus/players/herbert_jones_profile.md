# Herbert Jones — 2024-25 Scouting Profile

Jones is one of the more archetypal 3-and-D wings in the league and,
relative to his cost, remains undervalued outside of New Orleans. His
defensive versatility is the headline: he can credibly guard positions 1
through 4, has the length to contest without fouling, and his instincts as
a helper (deflections, weak-side steals) are elite for a low-usage role
player.

Offensively he has settled into a low-mistake, high-value role: quick
catch-and-shoot decisions from the corner, occasional relocation threes,
and very little dribble creation. His three-point percentage has climbed
each of the last two seasons as his shot selection has tightened almost
entirely to spot-up looks — he rarely takes a bad shot.

The ceiling on his offensive game is capped by a lack of shot creation off
the dribble, so he will likely never be more than a complementary scorer.
But for teams looking for a plug-and-play wing who defends the opponent's
best perimeter threat and doesn't need the ball to be effective, he is one
of the better value plays in the league relative to his current cost.
